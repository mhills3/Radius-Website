Why this might be a fit

Built around the player, not the scorecard.

Most apps show you what you did. Radius tells you what to do next — which disc to throw, which line to play, what to practice — based on how you actually throw. We're picky about who we partner with because the program is meant to feel like a real partnership, not a transaction.
